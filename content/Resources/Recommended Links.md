A curated collection of useful links, cool websites, tech blogs, and online resources for software engineering, design, and reference.

## Tech Blogs

- [Pinterest Engineering Blog](https://medium.com/pinterest-engineering) - Technical articles, architecture deep-dives, and engineering insights from Pinterest.
- [OpenAI Engineering Blog](https://openai.com/news/engineering/) - Technical updates, research breakthroughs, and engineering practices from OpenAI.

## Tutorials & Educational Resources

- [Lena Voita's Homepage](https://lena-voita.github.io/) - High-quality NLP lectures, course materials ("NLP Course for You"), and interactive explanations of deep learning concepts.
- [llama.cpp Guide](https://blog.steelph0enix.dev/posts/llama-cpp-guide/) - Comprehensive guide on setting up, understanding, and optimizing llama.cpp for local LLM inference.
- [Highly Opinionated Advice on How to Write ML Papers](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/eJGptPbbFPZGLpjsp/highly-opinionated-advice-on-how-to-write-ml-papers) - A LessWrong article offering structured, practical guidance on writing and structuring machine learning research papers.
