## Overview
**Company:** Abridge (Generative AI for healthcare, focused on structuring clinical notes from patient-clinician conversations).
**Role:** ML Infrastructure Engineer, Model Inference
**Focus:** Building and optimizing the core inference infrastructure that powers machine learning models to enhance scalability, efficiency, and performance.

## Key Responsibilities
- Design, deploy, and maintain scalable **Kubernetes** clusters for AI model inference and training.
- Develop and maintain high-performance, low-latency ML model serving infrastructure.
- Optimize compute-heavy workflows and maximize **GPU utilization**.
- Build robust model API orchestration systems.
- Collaborate with ML and product teams to scale backend infrastructure.

## Core Requirements & Skills
- Strong experience deploying ML models in production.
- Deep understanding of container orchestration and distributed systems.
- Expertise in **Kubernetes administration** (Custom Resource Definitions (CRDs), operators, cluster management).
- Experience managing distributed systems for batch and real-time workloads.

### Ideal Qualifications
- **Model Serving Frameworks:** NVIDIA Triton Server, VLLM, TRT-LLM.
- **ML Toolchains:** PyTorch, TensorFlow, distributed training/inference libraries.
- **Hardware Optimization:** GPU cluster management, CUDA optimization.
- **DevOps & Infrastructure:** Infrastructure as Code (Terraform, Ansible), GitOps practices.
- **Containers:** Image optimization, multi-stage builds for ML workloads.
- **Applications:** Orchestrating ASR (Automatic Speech Recognition) or LLM models.

## Areas for Self-Improvement & Study
Based on this job description, the following areas are critical for mastering ML Infrastructure and are worth studying further:
1. **Advanced Kubernetes:** [[Kubernetes]], focusing on administration, writing CRDs, and operators.
2. **Model Serving Frameworks:** Deep dive into [[vLLM]], [[NVIDIA Triton Server]], and [[TRT-LLM]].
